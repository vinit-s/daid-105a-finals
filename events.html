Events Along the Coast

Enjoy music, local markets, and time outdoors. These sample events show a few ways to spend a coastal weekend.

NOV 7

Seaside Makers Market

Capitola · 10 AM–2 PM

Browse handmade crafts and local artwork. Take a stroll through town and stop for a bite along the way.

NOV 14

Music by the Bay

Monterey · 2–5 PM

Spend an afternoon listening to live music by the water. Bring a blanket and enjoy a relaxed day with friends.

NOV 21

Community Beach Cleanup

Santa Cruz · 9–11 AM

Help care for the shoreline with a morning on the beach. Bring reusable gloves, water, and comfortable shoes.
