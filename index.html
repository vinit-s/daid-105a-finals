Explore the Coast

Discover California’s coast, from sandy beaches and rugged cliffs to lively waterfront towns. Follow scenic trails, explore tide pools, or stop for a meal with an ocean view. Whether you have an afternoon or a whole weekend, find a new place to slow down and enjoy the shoreline.

A Day by the Bay

Start your day with a walk beside the bay. Watch the waves roll in, feel the cool ocean breeze, and take a moment to enjoy the view before heading into town.

Stop at a local café for breakfast, then explore the shops and paths along the waterfront. Spend the afternoon on the sand or find a scenic overlook where you can sit and watch the boats pass by.

Finish with a relaxed meal and a sunset by the water. Ready to plan another day? Explore our places, food, events, and stays to build your own coastal getaway.

Visitor Tips

Plan Ahead

Check the weather before leaving and bring a light jacket. Coastal weather can change quickly.

Getting Around

Walking and public transportation are easy ways to explore many popular areas.

Take Your Time

Leave some extra time in your schedule to stop, explore, and enjoy the scenery.
